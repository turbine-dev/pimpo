# Governance

Vigia is free and open source, and it should outlive any one person.

## Roles

- **Contributors**: anyone who opens an issue or a pull request.
- **Maintainers**: can merge. Someone becomes a maintainer after reviewing well for three months, when two existing maintainers propose it and nobody objects within a week.
- **Security team**: the maintainers who receive private reports (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

## Decisions

- Day-to-day decisions happen in pull requests, by consensus of the reviewers.
- Safety, data format or governance changes go through an RFC (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- If consensus fails, maintainers vote. A simple majority wins; changes to this document need two thirds.

## Healthy project

The goal is at least three maintainers besides the founder, each reviewing ten or more pull requests a month, so no single person is required for releases, reviews or security fixes. Release and signing keys are held by at least two maintainers.

## Money

Vigia stays free: no paid tier, no telemetry, no hosted service required. Donations, if any, go to infrastructure and audits, and are reported publicly.

## Conduct

Everyone follows the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
