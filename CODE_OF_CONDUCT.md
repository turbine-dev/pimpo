# Code of Conduct

We want Pimpo's community to be welcoming to everyone, whatever their experience, background or language.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Criticize ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, or unwanted attention, in any channel of the project.
- Respect privacy: never share someone's personal data, or anything from their Pimpo, without consent.
- Help newcomers. A question is never a burden.

Report problems to the maintainers by opening an [issue](https://github.com/turbine-dev/pimpo/issues/new/choose); if it should not be public, use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment or profile involved, or the private [security report form](https://github.com/turbine-dev/pimpo/security/advisories/new) and say it is about conduct. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may warn, suspend, or remove anyone who breaks these rules, and they follow the same rules themselves.

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
