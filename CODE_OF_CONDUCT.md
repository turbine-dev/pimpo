# Code of Conduct

We want Zodim's community to be welcoming to everyone, whatever their experience, background or language.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Criticize ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, or unwanted attention, in any channel of the project.
- Respect privacy: never share someone's personal data, or anything from their Zodim, without consent.
- Help newcomers. A question is never a burden.

Report problems to the maintainers privately, at owner@example.com. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may warn, suspend, or remove anyone who breaks these rules, and they follow the same rules themselves.

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
